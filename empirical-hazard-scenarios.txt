================================================================================
Empirical hazard scenarios — Nykvarn (H2solo Ytterberga 2:5)
================================================================================

Purpose
  This file provides publishable, English empirical data for Section 6.1 of the
  study. It paraphrases and structures hazard narratives from a 2023 LBE risk
  assessment (Appendix E, Safety Partner, 2023-03-10) that cannot be
  redistributed with the paper. Content is interpreted to match the Table 3
  layout in the paper and the NYHKB instantiation (H2HOA).

Provenance
  Source: H2solo AB risk assessment and explosion protection documentation
  (Appendix E), scope sections 5.1–5.3 (technical room, piping, external
  building). Facility: on-site hydrogen production (~35 bar), compression to
  350 bar storage (1650 L COPV), return feed to fuel cell (~3–6 bar).

Disclaimer
  These scenarios are qualitative engineering summaries, not a full risk
  register. Likelihood and risk-reduction measures from the original analysis
  are omitted here; only elements needed for ontological instantiation are kept.
  Wording is the authors' interpretation for the research artefact.

Link to knowledge base (NYHKB)
  Each scenario has a stable ID (ES-*). Hazards, harmful events, and key
  situations in nyhkb.ttl (version 1.2.1) reference these IDs in rdfs:comment
  (search: "Empirical scenario ID"). The KB decomposes one empirical row into
  several typed individuals; the index lists primary hazards only.

Shared KB elements (not separate ES-* rows)
  Piping releases ES-PL-01, ES-PL-02, and ES-PL-03 share Event_LowPressureRelease_
  PipelineRoute and Event_HighPressureRelease_PipelineRoute in NYHKB. One release
  can result in jet (ES-PL-01), culvert deflagration (ES-PL-02), and corridor
  fireball (ES-PL-03) situations.

ES-TR-01 — Uncontrolled hydrogen release in the technical room
================================================================================
(Table 3 representative scenario; Appendix E section 5.1.2.1–5.1.2.2)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Technical room (equipment room) inside the existing
                        building: electrolyser, dryer, and fuel cell for on-site
                        hydrogen production and consumption at approximately
                        35 bar; no bulk storage; separate mechanical ventilation
                        and gas detection planned.

Hazard                  Uncontrolled release of hydrogen from production or
                        consumption equipment.

Cause                   Incorrect installation of hydrogen production or
                        consumption equipment, or external physical impact on
                        equipment (e.g. mechanical damage in the occupied room).

Hazardous situation     Formation of a flammable or explosive hydrogen–air
                        atmosphere in or near the confined equipment enclosure
                        after release. The original analysis judges formation
                        unlikely under normal design (small production rate,
                        vent routing outdoors, competent installation).

Hazardous event         Ignition of the explosive atmosphere by nearby ignition
                        sources (hot surfaces, static discharge, or electrical
                        sparking in the vicinity).

Consequence             Fire or explosion (jet fire, deflagration, or fireball per
                        Table 4) causing burn injury, blast/fragment effects, and
                        damage to people, equipment, and the building.

NYHKB (interpretation)  Location_TechnicalRoom; Event_LowPressureRelease_TechnicalRoom;
                        Situation_ExplosiveAtmosphere_TechnicalRoom,
                        Situation_FlammableCloud_TechnicalRoom;
                        Event_HotSurfaceIgnition_TechnicalRoom,
                        Event_StaticSparkIgnition_TechnicalRoom;
                        Hazard_JetFire_TechnicalRoom, Hazard_Deflagration_TechnicalRoom,
                        Hazard_Fireball_TechnicalRoom;
                        HarmfulEvent_JetFire_TechnicalRoom,
                        HarmfulEvent_Deflagration_TechnicalRoom,
                        HarmfulEvent_Fireball_TechnicalRoom; harm to personnel and property.

ES-TR-02 — Detonation under severe confinement (technical room)
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.1.4; low-probability extreme case)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Technical room with confined equipment layout.

Hazard                  Detonation of hydrogen–air mixture under severe
                        confinement (extreme scenario).

Cause                   High-energy ignition source acting on an explosive
                        atmosphere in unfavourable confinement.

Hazardous situation     Detonation conditions in the equipment space.

Hazardous event         High-energy ignition.

Consequence             Severe explosion with major injury, structural damage,
                        and equipment loss.

NYHKB (interpretation)  Situation_DetonationCondition_TechnicalRoom;
                        Event_HighEnergyIgnition_TechnicalRoom;
                        Hazard_Detonation_TechnicalRoom.

ES-PL-01 — Uncontrolled release from above-ground piping
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.2.2.1)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Hydrogen piping route between technical room and external
                        building (above ground, through building fabric); pressures
                        approximately 35 bar, 350 bar, and 3–6 bar on different legs.

Hazard                  Uncontrolled hydrogen release from piping.

Cause                   External mechanical impact on exposed piping (e.g. impact
                        during works or vehicle contact where applicable).

Hazardous situation     Hydrogen jet and/or flammable cloud along the piping corridor.

Hazardous event         Ignition by static electricity or hot surfaces near the leak.

Consequence             Jet fire threatening personnel and property; possible fire
                        spread toward adjacent building volumes.

NYHKB (interpretation)  Location_PipelineRoute; Component_H2Piping;
                        Event_HighPressureRelease_PipelineRoute or
                        Event_LowPressureRelease_PipelineRoute;
                        Situation_HydrogenJet_PipelineRoute;
                        Hazard_JetFire_PipelineRoute; HarmfulEvent_JetFire_PipelineRoute.

ES-PL-02 — Release and ignition in the pipe culvert
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.2.2.1)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Underground pipe culvert carrying hydrogen piping between
                        technical room and external building.

Hazard                  Uncontrolled release with accumulation in a confined culvert.

Cause                   Excavation damage, other external impact, or leakage on
                        the buried section.

Hazardous situation     Explosive atmosphere in the closed culvert volume with
                        potential pressure build-up before ignition.

Hazardous event         Ignition (e.g. static electricity).

Consequence             Deflagration or explosive overpressure; burn and blast
                        trauma; damage to infrastructure and surroundings.

                        Event_HighPressureRelease_PipelineRoute,

ES-PL-03 — Delayed ignition of dispersed cloud along piping route
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.2.2)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Piping corridor and surroundings between buildings.

Hazard                  Fireball from ignition of a dispersed hydrogen cloud after
                        delayed ignition.

Cause                   Release followed by delayed ignition when a flammable cloud
                        has spread along the route.

Hazardous situation     Flammable vapor cloud along the piping route.

Hazardous event         Ignition of the cloud.

Consequence             Fireball affecting personnel and property along the corridor.

NYHKB (interpretation)  Situation_FlammableCloud_PipelineRoute;
                        Hazard_Fireball_PipelineRoute; HarmfulEvent_Fireball_PipelineRoute.

ES-EB-CR-01 — Uncontrolled release in the compressor and consumption room
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.3.2.1, 5.3.2.3)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Compressor and consumption room in the external building:
                        compressor (35→350 bar), valves, safety relief paths, ATEX
                        equipment; explosive atmosphere judged likely in normal operation.

Hazard                  Uncontrolled hydrogen release with ignition of explosive
                        atmosphere (deflagration, jet fire, or fireball).

Cause                   Long-term wear, faulty installation, seal or connection
                        failure, or friction/heating at the compressor; unclassified
                        electrical equipment, static electricity, or hot surfaces.

Hazardous situation     Explosive or flammable atmosphere in the enclosed compressor room.

Hazardous event         Ignition (friction, hot surface, static spark, or escalation
                        from an initial fire).

Consequence             Explosion or jet fire; pressure effects; burn and fragmentation
                        injuries; fire spread to adjacent rooms and the neighbouring building.

NYHKB (interpretation)  Location_CompressorRoom;
                        Event_HighPressureRelease_CompressorRoom;
                        Situation_ExplosiveAtmosphere_CompressorRoom (DeflagrationCondition),
                        Situation_FlammableCloud_CompressorRoom,
                        Situation_HydrogenJet_CompressorRoom;
                        Hazard_Deflagration_CompressorRoom, Hazard_JetFire_CompressorRoom,
                        Hazard_Fireball_CompressorRoom (inheresIn compressor / room confinement);
                        HarmfulEvent_Deflagration_CompressorRoom,
                        HarmfulEvent_JetFire_CompressorRoom,
                        HarmfulEvent_Fireball_CompressorRoom.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Compressor and consumption room; connected 5 L portable

ES-EB-CR-03 — Pressure exposure from compressor operation
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.3.1.1)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Compressor and consumption room.

Hazard                  Pressure hazard associated with compressor and pressurised
                        piping (non-flammability mechanical hazard).

Cause                   Malfunction or failure of pressure-containing equipment.

Hazardous situation     (Not modelled as flammable atmosphere; pressure exposure

ES-EB-ST-01 — Mechanical damage to the stored hydrogen cylinder
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.3.2.1)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Dedicated hydrogen cylinder room (1650 L, 350 bar, Type IV COPV).

Hazard                  Uncontrolled high-pressure release from the storage cylinder.

Cause                   External mechanical impact damaging the cylinder shell or

ES-EB-ST-02 — External fire impinging on the hydrogen cylinder
================================================================================
(Appendix E section 5.3.2.2)

Element                 Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical location       Hydrogen cylinder room; adjacent compressor room or
                        neighbouring building fire as initiating context.

Hazard                  Thermal and pressure failure of the hydrogen cylinder due
                        to external fire heating.

Cause                   Fire in an adjacent room or nearby building heating the COPV
                        beyond relief capacity.

Hazardous situation     Equipment damage and rising vessel pressure / weakening.

Hazardous event         Rupture or catastrophic failure of the cylinder when pressure
                        relief is insufficient.

Consequence             Major explosion; serious injury; widespread property and
                        environmental damage over a large area.

NYHKB (interpretation)  Event_ExternalFire_CompressorRoom;
                        Situation_EquipmentDamage_CompressorRoom;
                        Event_Rupture_H2Cylinder → Situation_HydrogenJet_H2CylinderRoom;
                        Hazard_Thermal_H2Cylinder;
                        HarmfulEvent_Detonation_ExternalBuilding;
                        Harm_ThermalDamage_ExternalBuilding, Harm_Injury_Personell, Harm_Damage_Environment.
